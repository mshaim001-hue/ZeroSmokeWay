# 3-minute demo script

Record over a live phone-sized capture. Voice in English for judges, product UI may stay Russian. Do not wait until 4 November.

## 0:00–0:20 — Impact

Voice: “Quit-smoking apps already know cigarettes are harmful. That is not why people keep smoking. They keep a private story. I built ZeroSmokeWay so Meta Muse can find that story — not by lecturing, by walking someone through an image until they say it themselves.”

On screen: title card, then the Scene 1 idle screen.

## 0:20–1:00 — Cinema

Voice: “Each scene is a short film. Tap. No dragging a character. Act 1, scene 1: a bee drinks nectar, sure it can leave whenever it wants. The hairs only point down.”

On screen: play Scene 1 film in full.

## 1:00–2:30 — Muse leads

Voice: “When the film ends, the chat is the whole screen. There is no question bank. Muse Spark speaks first. It is not a chatbot wrapper: the model returns a decision — hold, probe, or open the next scene. I never fake a fallback line.”

On screen: live conversation. Type something like the first cigarette / “I’ll stop when I want.” Wait for the real reply. If the advance button appears, point to it: “The model just decided the person connected the image to their life. The client did not unlock the next scene by itself.”

## 2:30–3:00 — Next scene, new hero

Voice: “Scene 2 is a different creature: a hare at a salt well. The drink that ‘helps’ is salt. Same trap, new picture. That is the product: Muse chooses how to walk someone through their own reasons, scene by scene.”

On screen: tap Дальше, play a few seconds of the well film, freeze.

End card: public repo + deployed URL.
