# Act 1 · Scene 4 — The Tight Shoes Paradox

Date: 27 October 2026. Same loop: a short film, then a talk.

## Why

He sits in a room in shoes that are far too tight. The pain is constant. The only pleasure is taking them off for a moment. Then he puts them back, and the pain returns.

The talk is so they can see it themselves: the “pleasure” of a cigarette is only a short stop of the pain and itch the cigarette itself made.
