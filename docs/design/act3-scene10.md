# Act 3 · Scene 10 — The Swamp of Cutting Down

Date: 29 October 2026. Same loop: a short film, then a talk.

## Why

He decides to smoke only five a day. Time between cigarettes stretches like forever. Each one becomes hyper-precious and “tasty”. The withdrawal is stretched across a whole life.

The way out is seeing that cutting down is the cruelest torture.
