# Act 2 · Scene 6 — Bosses: Boredom and Concentration

Date: 28 October 2026. Same loop: a short film, then a talk.

## Why

He sits through a dull lecture with an important report still empty on the laptop. He lights a cigarette to wake up — or to focus. The same stick is sold as a stimulant for boredom and a sedative for stress.

The talk is the duel with that absurdity: one substance cannot both excite and relax. The cigarette only pulls him off the work and starves the brain of oxygen. The report stays blank.
