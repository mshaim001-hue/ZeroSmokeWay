ZeroSmokeWay

Not another lecture about how bad smoking is.

You already know. Quit-apps know it too. People keep smoking because they keep a private story: “I can stop whenever I want.” “It calms me.” “Just this one.”

ZeroSmokeWay is a mobile cinematic path. Each scene is a short film — a fairy-tale picture of the trap. After the film, Meta Muse Spark talks with you. It does not run a questionnaire. It stays with your words and decides whether to hold, probe, or open the next scene.

What it is not

  • Not a nicotine-replacement shop.
  • Not medical advice, and not a claim that it will make you quit.
  • Not a chatbot wrapper with a prepared list of questions.

How a scene works

You watch. Then the chat is the whole screen. Muse speaks first. The server — not the button on the phone — decides if you have tied the picture to your own smoking. Only then does the next film unlock.

Scene 1 is a bee in a pitcher plant, sure it can leave whenever it wants. Scene 13 is the last cigarette in a cell whose door is already open. The cigarette was the prison.

Carr, without the brand

Allen Carr’s Easyway is an influence: the trap is in the head, not a lack of willpower. This is not an official Carr product. The films are original. The talk is live.
