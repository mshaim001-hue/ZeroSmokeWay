ZeroSmokeWay

Not another lecture about how bad smoking is.

You already know cigarettes are harmful. ZeroSmokeWay is a mobile path of short films. After each film you talk about what felt familiar, what did not, and what smoking does in your day.

The talk stays with your words. It is not a questionnaire and not a score. When a scene’s talk is ready, you can watch the next film. After the last film you can confirm a last cigarette and open a home screen with a smoke-free count and a place to talk things through.

What it is not

  • Not a nicotine-replacement shop.
  • Not medical advice, and not a claim that it will make you quit.
  • Not a chatbot with a prepared list of questions.

How a scene works

You watch. Then the chat is the whole screen. You answer in your own words. The next film opens when that scene’s main question has been looked at — not because you pressed a skip button.

Scene 1 is a bee in a pitcher plant, sure it can leave whenever it wants. Later films look at stress, ordinary cues, and a last cigarette. The pictures are stories, not medical proof.

Carr, without the brand

Allen Carr’s Easyway is an influence: the trap is in the head, not a lack of willpower. This is not an official Carr product. The films are original. The talk is live.
